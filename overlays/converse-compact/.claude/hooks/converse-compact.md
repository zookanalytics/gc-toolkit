This session is a converse sitting. It hosts one conversation with the
operator about one bead, the conversation's subject, and it goes on from your
summary. Its record is the conversation's notes and the subject's notes, which
hold only what a command in this session wrote there. So, besides your usual
sections, the summary carries these four parts.

1. What the operator said that the record does not hold yet. Quote word for
   word, with their spelling and typos kept, each ruling, ask and correction
   the operator gave that no command in this session wrote to the
   conversation's notes or the subject's notes. Give each quote the time it
   was said when the session shows one. Quote rather than paraphrase, because
   the sitting acts on these words, and leave out what the record already
   holds.
2. Where the decision stands. State the decision that is still open, the
   recommendation as it last stood, and each option still offered, with what
   it costs. Then quote word for word the hand-back that ended the last
   message posted to the operator: its header, which names the subject, its
   body and its decision.
3. The record's names. Copy the CONV, CONVERSATION, SUBJECT and GATES lines
   exactly as the converse entry last printed them, and its WORK and AGREED
   lines when it printed those. Name each other bead, gate and pull request
   this sitting filed, sent or answered, and say what each one is.
4. What to do first. End the summary by telling the sitting to run the
   converse entry again before anything else, with the exact command this
   session ran it with, and to act on the ENTRY and NEXT lines it prints. The
   entry re-reads from the record where the sitting stands. When a sign-off in
   this session ran and a step after it has not landed, say which steps are
   still owed, whether the sign-off closed the conversation or handed it back
   to the operator's queue: the send or the close of the subject that some
   rulings make after the sign-off, and the hand-back, recorded and posted.
   Tell the sitting to take those steps before it drains, because the entry
   then finds no conversation and prints NEXT=drain.
