It now states that an approval persists across the commits that follow, that the dismiss-stale setting is off and stays off (it would drop an approval on a content-neutral rebase and make routine rebases far harder than they earn), and that the city dismisses only its own superseded machine review, never a human verdict (`assets/scripts/signoff.sh`, `dismiss_superseded`; `services/helm/README.md`).
